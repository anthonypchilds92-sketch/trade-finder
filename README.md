# Trade Finder

A personal dynasty trade finder for the Sleeper league **Futures Past + Present**.

When you open it, it loads live data from:
- **Sleeper**: rosters, records, traded picks, season stats and trending adds
- **FantasyCalc**: dynasty trade values for the league's format (10 teams, 2 QBs, half-PPR)

It then suggests trades that help a rebuilding team (The Two Tonys) and also suit the other side:
- sells players past their age peak, or whose value is mostly about this season
- targets young players and picks with breakout signals: youth, usage, scoring ahead of value, buzz, momentum
- checks that market values are close and that the partner's lineup or long-term value improves

Everything is in `index.html`. Settings, team strategies and Protect/Shop marks are saved in your browser.
